Swap plants with your neighbours

Leaf & Lend is a free monthly plant swap. Bring a spare cutting, seedling or repotted plant and go home with something new for your windowsill.

How it works

Label your plant with its name and light needs, place it on the swap table, and pick anything you like. You do not have to bring a plant to take part.

Good to swap

  • Cuttings
  • Seedlings
  • Herbs
  • Pots and trays

Next swap

First Saturday of the month, 10:00 to 12:00, at the community hall.
